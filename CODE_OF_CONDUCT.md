# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, background,
identity, experience level, nationality, or beliefs.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive, especially when you disagree.
- Assume good faith; ask before assuming bad intent.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully.
- Keep discussion on the technical topic.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Using the project to promote malware, stalkerware or surveillance of people without their consent.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and contributions that violate this code, and may ban
contributors who repeatedly or seriously do so. Report problems privately to the maintainers through the
repository's Security tab (private reporting) or by contacting the repository owner on GitHub.

This code is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(<https://www.contributor-covenant.org>).
