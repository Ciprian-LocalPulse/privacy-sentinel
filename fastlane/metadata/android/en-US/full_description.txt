Sentinel tells you when your camera or microphone is used, by which app (best guess), and whether it happened while your screen was off.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Live log of camera and microphone start/stop</li>
<li>Alerts for suspicious use with the screen off (calls excluded)</li>
<li>Probable app for each event (optional Usage access)</li>
<li>Audit of apps that can use the camera or microphone</li>
<li>Restarts after reboot</li>
</ul>

<b>Privacy</b>
No internet permission, no trackers, no ads. Everything stays on your phone.

<b>Limits</b>
Sentinel detects and reports. It cannot block access by system-level software or a compromised OS.
